WHO WE ARE

Building the next generation of student builders.

The AWS Student Builder Group at Central University Of Jammu is a student-led technical community dedicated to helping students explore cloud computing, DevOps, AI, and modern technology.

Our objective is to bridge the gap between academic learning and real world industry requirements through hands on workshops, technical sessions, collaborative projects, hackathons, and community activities.

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced learner, our community provides an environment to learn, experiment, build, collaborate, and grow together.

Learn. Build. Innovate.

Explore cloud technology and turn your ideas into practical projects.

OUR MISSION

We believe the best way to learn the cloud is to build in it. Every workshop, hackathon and lecture we run exists to turn curious students into confident builders who can design, deploy and scale real systems on AWS.

WHY JOIN US

Cloud Tournament

A gamified, team based cloud competition in the spirit of AWS GameDay and AWS Cloud Quest tournaments. Teams will work through live, sandboxed AWS labs and real-world scenario challenges covering services like EC2, S3, IAM and networking. Racing against the clock and each other on a shared leaderboard. No prior AWS experience is required, just curiosity and a willingness to build under pressure.

  • Online cloud labs
  • Live leaderboard
View Event November 2026

AWS Student Community Day

Modeled on AWS's own Community Day format held across cities in India every year, this will be our chapter's take on a full day of community led cloud learning. Expect technical talks on AWS services, generative AI and DevOps, hands on demo stations, and open conversations with AWS enthusiasts, alumni and community builders. A peer to peer event where students learn from students as much as from speakers.
